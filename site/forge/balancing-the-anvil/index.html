Balancing the Anvil

This page shows ways to mix and match plans across vendors, from free to all-in. Balancing the anvil means finding the models that work best for you, at a price that fits your budget and your workflow. For the models themselves, see Models.

Find Your Balance

Many people lean on one vendor, Anthropic or OpenAI. Each ladder puts one of them first and climbs from free to all-in. To stay in one lane, follow that vendor's row. To mix and match, follow the full tiers, which add a plan from the other vendor as they climb. If you are still deciding, begin at Starter, where both are free. Free plans change often, so check the vendor's page before you count on one.

Plan Notes

Anthropic Claude Pro costs $20/month or $200/year. The tables use the annual rate, which saves $40.

OpenAI ChatGPT Pro 500 costs $500/month and falls outside these tiers.

Google AI Plus costs $4.99/month or $49.99/year, rounded to $5 and $50.

Google AI Pro costs $19.99/month or $199.99/year, rounded to $20 and $200.

Google AI Ultra costs $99.99/month for 5x limits and $199.99/month for 20x limits, rounded to $100 and $200. Google bills Ultra monthly only, so the yearly column shows 12 months.

Gemini Model Availability: In the Gemini app, Gemini 4 Hydrogen is on every plan, AI Pro adds Gemini 4 Neon, and AI Ultra adds Gemini 4 Carbon. In Antigravity, Free and AI Plus run Gemini 3.8 Flash only, AI Pro adds Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Gemini 4 Neon, and AI Ultra adds Gemini 4 Carbon. See plans and Antigravity models.

JetBrains All Products Pack includes AI Pro with 10 AI Credits every 30 days for personal use. See credit details.
